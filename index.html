Personal WHOOP Connection — Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 24, 2026. This notice describes the intended manual prototype; it will be updated before new features are enabled.

This application is intended solely for its owner's personal use on their own Mac. It is not offered to other users.

Purpose and data

After explicit WHOOP authorization, the application will request read-only access to sleep and recovery data to display one completed night's sleep and associated recovery locally. This may include recovery score, sleep performance and stage durations, HRV, resting heart rate, timestamps and record identifiers needed to match records. It will not request profile, workout or body-measurement permissions.

Storage and security

Credentials and tokens will be held in macOS Keychain, with no cloud synchronization requested. Transient processing requires process memory. The initial manual prototype will not save raw health responses or a health-data history. Credentials, tokens and health-response contents will not be included in application logs. No background collection will be enabled without separate approval.

Sharing

The Mac will communicate directly with WHOOP for authorization and data retrieval. No intermediary data relay will be used. A summary may be shared with OpenAI for review at the owner's direction, under the applicable OpenAI terms and privacy settings. This does not require a separately billed OpenAI API. No credentials or tokens will be included in any summary.

Control

The owner may revoke the integration in WHOOP and remove its local Keychain items. Closing the manual review ends its in-memory display; no permanent health-data archive is intended. Retention and any later automation will be reviewed against WHOOP's terms before enabling them.

Public notice hosting

Only this general privacy notice will be published. Health data, credentials, tokens and OAuth callback URLs containing authorization codes must never be uploaded to its host. The hosting provider may process ordinary page-visit metadata under its own privacy policy.
